## Rules for every pack
- **One song, five versions.** All five share tempo, key, chords and length (the game
  crossfades between them mid-bar). Intensity comes from *arrangement*: which
  instruments play, how busy they are, how loud. Never from changing the tempo.
- **Built for hours of play.** Groove first, no shrill high parts, nothing that tires
  the ear. Lessons so far: Crescendo v1 was too high and harsh; v2 was too calm.
- **Loop of 16-32 bars**, ending so it flows back into bar 1.
- Tempo and level can still be fine-tuned in game: Odeum's per-pack Volume and Speed
  sliders (speed shifts pitch too, like a record player).

## The five moments: a shared base, read through each pack's style
| Moment | Base | Crescendo (electro swing) | Vesper (bossa / lounge) | Basilicus (Vegas swing) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Main** | the song's home, steady groove | four-on-the-floor + guitar pompe + clarinet melody | guitar + bass + brushes, flute/sax melody | lounge combo: vibes, muted trumpet, finger snaps |
| **Packs** (Tarot etc.) | curious, mystical | kick drops out, muted trumpet melody + vibes | melody to vibraphone, sparse, dreamy | organ + vibes, mysterious "who's behind the curtain" |
| **Planet** | floating, wide | no drums: strings + vibes over guitar and bass | pads + guitar only, slow chords, airy | strings + harp glissandos, glamorous |
| **Shop** | relaxed, fun browsing | the groove with a piano melody | easy groove, the most "café" version | casino floor: Hammond organ, cocktail piano |
| **Boss** | tense, driving | **the drop**: trumpet + sax + clarinet, synth bass, brass stabs | darker minor turn, busier percussion | the big show: full brass fanfare, strings, drum fills |

## Packs

### Crescendo: electro swing
- **Feel:** Caravan Palace / Parov Stelar: swing melodies and gypsy-jazz rhythm over a
  modern four-on-the-floor beat. The boss version is the "drop".
- **Tempo:** about **124 BPM** (electro swing sits around 110-130).
- **Instruments:** clarinet / muted trumpet / sax melody, gypsy-jazz "pompe" rhythm
  guitar, upright bass + offbeat synth bass, kick on every beat, claps on 2 and 4,
  swung hi-hats, brass stabs.
- **History:** v1 synth, too high and harsh; v2 soft synth, too calm; v3 classic
  Basie big band, not the big band Locked meant. Electro swing is the target.

### Vesper: bossa nova + late-night lounge blend
- **Feel:** the evening star; elegant, warm, unhurried. Bossa rhythm (nylon guitar,
  two-beat bass, brushes) dressed in lounge colours (Rhodes, tenor sax, vibes).
- **Tempo:** **100-120 BPM** for classic bossa; lounge-leaning bossa sits nearer 90-115.
- **Instruments:** nylon-string guitar (bossa rhythm), upright bass on a two-beat feel,
  brushed drums / shaker, Rhodes, flute or tenor sax, vibraphone.

### Basilicus: Las Vegas casino swing
- **Feel:** the royal jackpot; showtime, glitz, Rat Pack.
- **Tempo:** medium swing, about **120-135 BPM**: slower and slicker than Crescendo,
  so the two swing packs don't sound alike.
- **Instruments:** muted trumpet, vibraphone, Hammond organ, brass stabs, lush strings,
  finger snaps, cocktail piano.
- **Different from Crescendo:** lounge combo and showbiz glamour vs a big band that builds.
